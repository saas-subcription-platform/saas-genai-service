SAAS SUBSCRIPTION PLATFORM
==========================

PLATFORM OVERVIEW
-----------------

The SaaS Subscription Platform helps organizations manage their
subscriptions, employees, billing, payments, and employee
productivity.

The platform has three types of users:

- Public visitors
- Company Administrators
- Employees

COMMON ADMINISTRATOR FEATURES
-----------------------------

Company Administrator functionality is available across the
subscription plans.

Administrators can:

- Access the Admin Dashboard
- Manage employees/users
- Manage company profile
- Manage subscriptions
- View payment information
- View invoices
- View notifications
- Manage account/application settings
- Access Help


USER MANAGEMENT
---------------

Administrators can:

- View employees
- Add employees
- View employee details
- Edit employee information

Administrators can manage information such as:

- Name
- Email
- Phone
- Department
- Role
- Status

Supported roles include:

- Administrator
- Employee
- Manager


COMPANY PROFILE
---------------

Administrators can manage company information including:

- Company name
- Company phone
- GSTN
- PIN code
- Address
- Country
- Company size


SUBSCRIPTION MANAGEMENT
----------------------

Administrators can:

- View the current subscription
- View the current plan
- View subscription status
- View billing cycle
- View subscription dates
- View renewal information
- View available plans
- Change subscription plans
- Renew subscriptions
- Proceed with subscription payments


PAYMENTS
--------

Administrators can view payment-related information associated
with their subscriptions.

The platform supports online subscription payments.


INVOICES
--------

Administrators can view subscription invoices.

They can:

- View invoice information
- View billing periods
- View payment status
- View invoice amounts
- Open invoice details
- Download invoices


NOTIFICATIONS
-------------

Administrators can view notifications containing important
platform or organization-related updates.


SETTINGS
--------

Administrators can access account and application settings and
view their account information.


HELP
----

The platform provides a Help section for administrators.


EMPLOYEE MODULES
----------------

The platform provides four employee modules across the available
subscription plans:

1. Timesheet Management
2. Leave Management
3. Goals & Performance
4. Team Collaboration


TIMESHEET MANAGEMENT
--------------------

Timesheet Management helps employees record and manage their
working hours.

Employees can:

- Create timesheets
- Record their work
- Add projects and tasks
- Record hours worked
- Add descriptions
- Save their work
- Submit timesheets
- View their timesheets
- View timesheet details
- Edit timesheets that are still in progress

The module helps employees maintain records of their daily and
weekly work.


LEAVE MANAGEMENT
----------------

Leave Management helps employees manage their leave.

Employees can:

- View their leave balance
- Apply for leave
- Select a leave type
- Select leave dates
- Provide a reason
- View leave history
- Check the status of leave requests

Available leave types:

- Casual Leave
- Sick Leave
- Earned Leave


GOALS & PERFORMANCE
-------------------

Goals & Performance helps employees set and manage professional
goals and monitor their progress.

Employees can:

- View goals
- Create goals
- View goal details
- Update goals
- Track goal progress

The module provides an overview of:

- Active goals
- Completed goals
- Overdue goals
- Overall progress


TEAM COLLABORATION
------------------

Team Collaboration helps employees communicate and work together.

Employees can:

- Communicate with other employees
- Participate in company-wide conversations
- Have one-to-one conversations
- Create teams
- View teams
- View team members
- Search for employees
- Send messages
- View conversations

The module provides a shared space for team communication and
collaboration.

CHATBOT KNOWLEDGE BOUNDARY
--------------------------

The chatbot should only provide information about the SaaS
Subscription Platform and the features described in this
knowledge base.

It must not invent:

- Features
- Subscription plans
- Prices
- Modules
- Capabilities
- Services

If information about a platform feature is not available in this
knowledge base, the chatbot should state that it does not have
that information.

If a user asks an unrelated question, the chatbot should explain
that it is designed to answer questions about the SaaS Subscription
Platform.
